About Me

I'm an engineering manager and product person from Belgrade, Serbia, with an MSc in Computer Science (I graduated in cryptography). I started on large information systems, moved to iOS when the App Store opened in 2008, and since then I've led teams as CTO and engineering manager and owned products as a product manager.

With a team, I set clear goals, hire carefully, give honest feedback and keep releases going out on a steady schedule. On the product side I own the roadmap, define the KPIs and event tracking for each feature, and decide with design and engineering what to build next. I still write code, these days with AI tools across the whole development cycle.
